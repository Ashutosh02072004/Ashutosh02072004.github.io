FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Strike Squad free?

Yes, completely free. Ads appear only between offline matches, and you can choose to watch an ad to double your coins. All items are cosmetic, so nobody can pay to win.

Is the APK safe to install?

Yes — it's the official build, signed by Ashutosh Games and downloaded straight from our GitHub release. Android shows a warning for every app installed outside a store; that's normal. Only download it from this page.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every mode works offline against bots (Easy, Normal or Hard). You only need internet for online matches.

How do I play with friends?

Tap ONLINE. Use QUICK PLAY to join a public match, or HOST a room and share the code — friends type it and tap JOIN.

How do I update the game?

The game tells you when a new version is out. Download the new APK from this page and install it over the old one — your progress is kept. Don't uninstall first, or you'll lose your coins and skins.

Will it run on my phone?

It needs Android 7.0 or newer. Older or low-end phones start in LOW graphics automatically, and you can switch between 30 and 60 FPS in Settings.

Is it available in Hindi?

Yes — Settings → Language → हिंदी.
