Strike Squad — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Developer: Ashutosh Games · Contact: btechcsymca@gmail.com

Summary

  • Strike Squad does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress (coins, level, skins, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers to show and measure ads.
  • Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads, and can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to GameAnalytics. You can switch this off in Settings → Share anonymous stats & crash reports.

Information stored on your device

Your in-game name (callsign), chosen skin, coins, experience level, unlocked items, match statistics and settings are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from the IP address, in order to deliver, personalise (where permitted) and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

Gameplay statistics and crash reports (GameAnalytics)

To find bugs and improve the game we use GameAnalytics. The game sends: a random player ID created on first launch (not linked to your name, email or advertising ID), session start/length, matches played (mode, map, result, kills), tutorial progress, levels reached, in-game coins earned and spent, which ads were shown, average frame rate, error messages from the game, and basic device information (device model, operating system version, game version). GameAnalytics also receives your IP address when the data is sent, which it uses only to estimate your country.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous stats & crash reports → OFF; after that nothing more is sent. GameAnalytics privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Online multiplayer

When you play online, your callsign, chosen skin and in-game actions are sent to the match host or game server so other players can see you. Game servers see your IP address to maintain the connection. We do not store match data after the match ends, apart from temporary server logs (join/leave events) used to keep the service running, which are deleted regularly.

Children

Strike Squad is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for teens.

Your choices and rights

  • Change ad consent: Settings → Privacy options (shown where required by law).
  • Stop gameplay statistics and crash reports: Settings → Share anonymous stats & crash reports → OFF.
  • Delete local data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Request deletion of your gameplay statistics: see Delete your data.
  • Questions or requests (including EU/UK GDPR and India DPDP rights): contact btechcsymca@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
