Strike Squad — Delete your data

App: Strike Squad: Jetpack Shooter · Developer: Ashutosh Games · Contact: btechcsymca@gmail.com

How to request deletion

  1. Open Strike Squad → Settings (gear icon). At the bottom of the right panel you'll see your Player ID (for example Player ID: 3f9a2c1d).
  2. Send an email to btechcsymca@gmail.com with the subject "Strike Squad - Delete my data" and include your Player ID.
  3. We delete the data linked to that ID within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Strike Squad has no accounts, so the Player ID is how we find your data. You can also stop all future analytics at any time in Settings → Share anonymous stats & crash reports → OFF.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Gameplay statistics and crash reports (GameAnalytics) linked to your Player IDDeleted within 30 days of your request.
Your progress (coins, level, skins, settings, missions)Stored only on your phone. Delete it yourself by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Strike Squad → Storage → Clear storage).
Player name in online-server logs (join/leave lines)Not linked to any account; logs are rotated and deleted automatically within 30 days.
Advertising data (Google AdMob)Controlled by Google. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses data.
